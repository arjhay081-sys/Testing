About Bobby's Motorbike Rental

Your Trusted Partner for Island Adventures

Our Story

Bobby's Motorbike Rental started because Rogelito saw a real problem: tourists arriving in Santa Fe with no good way to get around independently. Tricycles run fixed routes. Habal-habal drivers don't always know the best spots. And renting from strangers with no address, no shop, and no accountability meant visitors had no protection if something went wrong.

As the Barangay Captain of Talisay, Rogelito decided to build something the community could be proud of — a legitimate, registered rental business where every bike is properly maintained, every customer gets real local knowledge, and every transaction is transparent and fair. Bobby's Motorbike Rental was born from that purpose, and that commitment hasn't changed since day one.

We're based in Barangay Talisay, Santa Fe — a short walk from the main port area, easy to find whether you're coming from the ferry or from your resort. We're open 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily, 7 days a week. No appointment needed. Walk in, pick your bike, and start exploring.

Meet the BMR Team

Bobby's Motorbike Rental is run by the BMR Team — a small, tight-knit family crew based in Talisay. Everyone on the team is local to Bantayan Island, which means when you ask us where to eat, which beach to go to, or what road conditions are like after rain, you're getting a real answer from someone who actually lives here — not a scripted recommendation from a tourist pamphlet.

We handle everything ourselves: bike maintenance, safety checks before each rental, customer pickups, and roadside help when someone needs it. Because the team is small and family-run, you're never dealing with a call center or an anonymous staff member. When you contact us on WhatsApp, you're talking to the same people who will hand you the keys.

From bike preparation and safety checks to assisting you on the road, our team is always ready to help with a smile. When you rent from us, you're supported by people who genuinely care about your safety and enjoyment.
